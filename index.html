Welcome to AlUla, Saudi Arabia

In the north-west of Saudi Arabia, between rose-hued sandstone cliffs and the ruins of ancient kingdoms, AlUla has been a crossroads of civilisation for more than two thousand years. Nabataean traders, Dadanite kings, and Lihyanite scribes all passed through this oasis valley — and their presence is still legible in the rock.

✦

Over five days, move from the UNESCO-listed tombs of Hegra to the open-air inscriptions of Jabal Ikmah, from a Michelin-curated lunch in the restored old town to a gourmet dinner beneath one of Arabia’s clearest night skies. AlUla is not a resort destination. It is a place where the scale of history makes the present feel very quiet.

Day 02 · Monday · Heritage

Ancient Wonders — Hegra, Dadan & Elephant Rock

BLD
After breakfast, a full day moving through AlUla’s most significant archaeological sites and natural landmarks. Hegra Vintage Land Rover Tour — Begin at the Hegra Visitor Centre and embark on a guided Land Rover tour through Hegra (Mada’in Saleh) — Saudi Arabia’s first UNESCO World Heritage Site. More than a hundred Nabataean tombs are carved into rose-coloured sandstone here, including the Tomb of Lihyan, Son of Kuza. The civilisation that built them controlled the incense trade routes between southern Arabia and the Mediterranean world. Approx. 2 hours Lunch at Jontoos — Dar Tantora, The House Hotel — A restored mudbrick property in AlUla Old Town. Chef Jaume Puigdengolas presents a refined take on Saudi-inspired cuisine using local ingredients and traditional techniques. Maraya Photo Stop — A brief stop at the world’s largest mirrored building, which reflects the surrounding desert cliffs across its 9,740 square metres of glass facade. Dadan & Jabal Ikmah Land Rover Tour — Explore the former capitals of the Dadanite and Lihyanite kingdoms. Jabal Ikmah is a canyon wall covered in inscriptions, dedications, and rock art spanning multiple scripts and centuries — the most concentrated open-air library of pre-Islamic Arabian writing in existence. Approx. 1.5 hours Elephant Rock at Sunset — Arrive at Jabal AlFil as the afternoon light falls across the valley. The formation stands twelve metres high, shaped by millennia of erosion into the outline of an elephant. Time at leisure here before moving on. Dinner at Somewhere — A stylish outdoor restaurant offering modern Mediterranean cooking, set among sandstone cliffs with open evening skies above.

BD

Return to the resort for breakfast and a relaxed morning. The day is yours — use the resort pool, book a treatment at the Habitas Thuraya Spa, or take a short walk into the surrounding landscape. The combination of the terrain, the silence, and the sky is the defining experience of AlUla after dark.

Day 04 · Wednesday · Leisure

A Day at Your Own Pace

B
Breakfast at the resort. The day is entirely at leisure — no fixed schedule, no transfers. AlUla Old Town — The old town is worth an unhurried morning. Inhabited continuously from before the 12th century until the early 1980s, it holds 900 houses, 500 shops and five town squares. A Rawi (local guide) can lead you through the maze of original stone and mudbrick buildings, or you can walk it independently. The AlUla Fort — dating to the 10th century — is visible from most of the town. The afternoon is free. Lunch and dinner are at your own arrangement from AlUla’s growing selection of restaurants and cafes.

Before You Go

Important Notes

AlUla Flights
Direct flights between Dubai (DXB) and AlUla (ULH) operate via flydubai and Saudia. Flight schedules vary by season. Confirm your departure dates with your OV advisor before finalising the itinerary, as airport transfer timings are built around your actual flight times.
Saudi Entry & Visa
Most nationalities travelling on GCC-linked itineraries or holding UAE residency can obtain a Saudi eVisa online. Requirements vary by passport. Your OV advisor will confirm the entry requirements applicable to your nationality well in advance of travel.
Balloon Flight
The hot air balloon experience on Day 3 is subject to weather and wind conditions on the morning. The operator makes a final decision by 05:00 on the day. If the flight does not operate, an alternative activity will be arranged and costs adjusted accordingly.
Dress & Culture
AlUla is an active heritage and tourism destination within Saudi Arabia. Respectful dress is appropriate at archaeological sites and in the old town — covering shoulders and knees. Both hotels are international properties with relaxed dress codes at their pools and restaurants.
Check-In & Check-Out
Standard hotel check-in is 14:00. Standard check-out is 12:00. On Day 1, the resort will arrange luggage storage and access to facilities prior to room readiness. Late check-out on Day 5 is subject to availability and may incur additional fees.
Gratuities
Gratuities for guides, drivers and restaurant staff are at your discretion and not included in the itinerary costs. We recommend presenting guide and driver tips in an envelope at the conclusion of each day as a token of appreciation.
